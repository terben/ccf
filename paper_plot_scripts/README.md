Scripts to reproduce the paper figures of section 5. The current paper figures use the Open Journal of Astrophysics (OJA) layout.

- `style.py`                 : matplotlib configuration for supported journal layouts (A&A and OJA)
- `figure_geometry.py`       : Figure of paper section 5.1
- `figure_roundtrip.py`      : Figure of paper section 5.2
- `figure_gaussianization.py`: Figure of paper section 5.3
